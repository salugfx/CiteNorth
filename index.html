Modern GEO platforms track mentions across answer engines, while technical auditors check whether a site gives those systems usable public evidence. CiteNorth starts with the second job so the free MVP stays deterministic and inexpensive.

DISCOVERABILITY

Can crawlers reach the useful parts?

Robots rules, sitemap presence, HTTPS, canonical signals and indexable markup form the technical floor.

UNDERSTANDING

Is the entity explicit?

Organization, Product or SoftwareApplication schema plus clear titles, headings and metadata reduce ambiguity.

ANSWERABILITY

Is there enough useful text to cite?

Structured sections, direct explanations and substantial server-rendered copy make extraction easier.

HONEST LIMITS

Readiness is not visibility.

An excellent technical score does not prove an answer engine currently recommends you. Real visibility tracking requires repeated prompts across engines and time.

ACTIONABLE OUTPUT

Fix the plumbing before buying more prompts.

The audit is designed to surface cheap, concrete improvements — missing schema, blocked crawlers, weak metadata, absent sitemaps and thin public copy.
