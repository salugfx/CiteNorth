Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026.

What we process

When you run an audit, the server fetches the public URL you provide plus selected public root files such as robots.txt, sitemap.xml and llms.txt. Contact form submissions are processed by Formspree.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the public tool. Avoid submitting confidential information in URLs or contact messages.

Analytics and advertising

This starter build does not include an analytics tracker or an AdSense publisher ID. If analytics or advertising is added later, this policy should be updated before deployment and any required consent controls should be implemented for applicable regions.

Retention

The included serverless functions do not implement a user database. Hosting platforms and Formspree may retain operational logs or form submissions under their own policies.

Your choices

You may choose not to use the scanner or contact form. For privacy questions, use the homepage contact form.
