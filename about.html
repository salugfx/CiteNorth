About CiteNorth

CiteNorth is an independent website-readiness product for teams adapting to AI-mediated discovery. The free audit focuses on technical and content signals that can be observed directly from public webpages. It does not claim access to private ranking systems and does not guarantee inclusion in AI-generated answers.

Product principles

Useful evidence comes before decorative scoring. Important limitations should be visible in the interface, and free tools should provide genuine value before asking users to pay.
